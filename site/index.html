Thank-you stickers and insert cards, printed at home.

Pick a shape and size, start from a ready-made design or bring your own art, and Robin fills the sheet for you. Print on sticker paper, pre-cut label sheets or cardstock, or send it to your Cricut.

Download for Windows Source code

Free and open source (MIT) for Windows 10 and 11. The download runs without installing; prefer an installer? Get the installer.

The app isn't code-signed yet, so your browser or Windows may warn you. In the browser, choose Keep; if Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.

How it works

  1. Choose a shape and size

    Circles from 1.5 to 3 in, squares, rectangles, and insert cards up to 5 × 7 in. Squares and rectangles can have rounded corners.

  2. Make it yours

    Start from one of ten thank-you designs. Change the words, colours, font and border, or add your shop name, your logo or a finished design of your own.

  3. Print or cut

    Robin fits as many as it can on each sheet, with crop marks for a paper trimmer. Print, save a PDF, or export a picture for Cricut Print Then Cut.

Counts are for plain paper, cardstock and full-sheet sticker paper. For pre-cut sticker sheets, type in your sheet's measurements and Robin lines up with it. A printer adjustment moves everything if your printer prints a little off.

Design your own

Click Design template to save a blank artboard for the size you picked. Open it in Canva, Inkscape, Affinity or Illustrator, design inside the guides, export a PNG and add it to Robin. Your design prints exactly where the template said it would.

Your designs stay on your computer

Robin has no network access, no accounts and no analytics. It only reads the pictures you give it and only writes the files you save.
